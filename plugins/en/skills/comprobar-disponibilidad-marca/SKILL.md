# Check trademark availability in Spain

## What it does
It guides a basic search to find out whether a brand, business, or product name is already in use or registered in Spain, before the user spends money registering it.

## Before starting, ask
- The exact name they want to use (and whether it has variants: with or without an accent mark, with or without a space).
- What the business does (a trademark only protects in the classes of goods or services that are chosen, not in all of them).
- Whether they want protection only in Spain or also in the European Union.

## Step by step
1. Search for the name as is in the trademark search tool of the OEPM (Spanish Patent and Trademark Office, Oficina Española de Patentes y Marcas), at oepm.es, in the trademark search section.
2. Also search the EUIPO search tool (euipo.europa.eu) if protection across the whole EU is of interest, not just Spain.
3. Check whether the name already exists as a company name in the Central Commercial Registry (Registro Mercantil Central, rmc.es), in the company-name search section.
4. Check whether the .es domain is available at dominios.es or any registrar, and whether the .com is also available.
5. Look at whether the name is in use as a username or page on the social networks where the business will operate.
6. Note the results: free, taken by something similar, or taken exactly.

## What to return
A simple table with: where you searched, what you found, and whether the name appears free or there is a risk of conflict. If there is a risk, say so clearly and explain why (identical or very similar name in the same class of business).

## What NOT to do
- Do not say "it's free" just because it does not show up on Google. The official registries must be checked.
- Do not confuse checking that the domain is free with checking that the trademark is free: they are different things and both must be checked.
- Do not present this as a legal guarantee. It is an indicative search; the final registration is assessed by the OEPM when processing it, and there may be similar trademarks that a search tool does not catch well. If the business is serious, recommend an industrial property agent or a specialized lawyer before investing in the brand.
