## What it does
It collects the quarter's issued and received invoices, separates output VAT from input VAT, calculates the difference to pay or offset, and estimates the IRPF installment payment (Form 130) if the client is taxed under direct estimation.

## What to ask before starting
Ask for the client's VAT regime (general, equivalence surcharge, cash basis), their IRPF estimation method (simplified direct or objective/modules), and whether they have payments on account from other quarters pending offset.
Also ask whether they will deliver the invoices as PDFs, in an Excel file, or from an invoicing program, because the format changes the step by step.

## Step by step
1. Gather all the quarter's issued invoices and add up the taxable bases and the output VAT amounts, separated by rate (4%, 10%, 21%).
2. Gather the deductible received invoices and add up the input VAT; discard expenses without a complete invoice (no tax ID/NIF, no VAT breakdown) because the Spanish tax authority (Hacienda) does not accept them.
3. Calculate the difference between output VAT and input VAT. If it is positive, it is what must be paid on Form 303; if it is negative, it is offset or a refund is requested if it is the last quarter.
4. If the client is under direct estimation, calculate 20% of the quarter's net income for Form 130, subtracting the payments already made in earlier quarters of the year.
5. Flag the doubtful invoices (mixed expenses, no breakdown, a tax ID that does not match) in a separate list for the client or their gestoría (tax and bookkeeping advisory) to review before filing anything.
6. Return a summary with the figures for each form and the list of invoices used, so the client or their advisor can check it.

## What to return and in what format
A table with: taxable base by VAT rate, output VAT, input VAT, result of Form 303, and estimated base and amount for Form 130. Below it, the list of excluded invoices and why.

## How to turn this into a paid service
It works as the basis for a fixed-price "quarterly close" service for freelancers who do not want to pay for a full gestoría: you charge for reviewing and leaving the figures ready, not for filing the form. ## What NOT to do
Do not file Form 303 or Form 130 with the Spanish Tax Agency (Agencia Tributaria) on the client's behalf: that requires a digital certificate or power of attorney, and if you make a mistake the liability and penalties fall on the taxpayer. Do not accept an invoice without verifying the tax ID (NIF), date, and VAT breakdown. Do not assume the client's tax regime; always ask, because objective and direct estimation are calculated differently. Do not replace the judgment of a registered tax advisor in complex cases (start of activity, cessation, intra-EU transactions): refer those cases.
