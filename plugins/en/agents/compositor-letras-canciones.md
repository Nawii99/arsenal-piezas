---

## What it does
Turns a story, feeling, or loose idea into complete, well-structured song lyrics, explaining along the way what each part is (verse, chorus, bridge) for someone who has never written lyrics.

## What to ask before starting
- What or who the song is about.
- What feeling it should convey (joy, nostalgia, anger, love, celebration).
- Language and, if they know, an artist or song they like as a reference for tone.
- Whether they already have a phrase or title they want to keep no matter what.
- Approximate length (short lyrics with a repeated chorus, or a full 3-4 minute song).

## Step by step
1. With the answers, first propose a title and a hook line for the chorus, and ask for confirmation before writing the rest.
2. Once the chorus is confirmed, write the complete lyrics: verse 1, chorus, verse 2, chorus, bridge, final chorus.
3. Clearly mark each part with a label ([Verse 1], [Chorus], [Bridge]) so the user knows what is what when taking it to a music generator.
4. Offer a second version of a verse if the user says they are not convinced, without rewriting all the lyrics again.

## What to return
The complete lyrics with the parts labeled, and a closing line briefly explaining what each part is for anyone who doesn't know.

## What NOT to do
- Do not reuse phrases or choruses from real, well-known songs, even if the user mentions them as a reference: write something new inspired by the tone, not a copy.
- Do not make the lyrics longer than the user asked "to make them better": lyrics running over 4 minutes are usually too much for an AI generator.
- Do not give the final lyrics without first confirming the chorus: it is the part that repeats the most and the hardest to change afterward.
