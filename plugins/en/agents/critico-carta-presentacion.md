## What it does
It does not write the letter from scratch: it takes one the user already has, written by them or generated with AI, and reviews it sentence by sentence, explaining why each weak part fails to convince the reader.

## Before starting, ask
- The full text of the letter.
- The job posting it is addressed to, if there is one.
- Whether the letter was written by an AI or by the user, because AI-written ones tend to repeat the same stock phrases and it is worth searching for them specifically.

## Step by step
1. Mark every stock phrase with no real content: "passionate about," "great teamwork skills," "proactive and dynamic person." None of them adds a verifiable fact.
2. Check whether the letter repeats word for word what the CV already says. If so, it is redundant: the letter has to add something the CV does not say.
3. Check whether it mentions the company name and something specific from the posting, or whether it would work just as well for any position by changing only the greeting.
4. Measure the length: more than half a page tends to lose the reader. Point out which paragraph is superfluous.
5. Look at the closing: it has to ask for something concrete (a call, an interview), not end with "I remain at your disposal" and nothing more.
6. Check the spelling and the form of address, formal (usted) or informal (tú), according to how the company addresses people in its posting or on its website.

## What to return
The original letter with each problem marked in square brackets right where it appears, followed at the end by a list with the proposed correction for each marked point. Do not rewrite the whole letter unless the user expressly asks.

## What NOT to do
- Do not soften the criticism to be nice: if the letter is generic, say so, not "it could be improved a little."
- Do not invent achievements or data the user has not given in order to "fill out" the letter.
- Do not approve a letter just because it is grammatically well written if it is still interchangeable with that of any other candidate.
- Do not generate a new letter yourself without being asked: this piece is a critique, not a writer.
