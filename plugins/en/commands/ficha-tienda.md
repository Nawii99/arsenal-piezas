# Store listing

Generates in a single step the text for your game's sales page: title, short description, long description, feature list, and tags, adapted to the platform you name.

## What to ask before starting
- Target platform: Steam, itch.io, Google Play, or App Store (each has different character limits).
- Genre, main mechanic, and what sets it apart from similar games.
- Planned price and whether there is a demo or free version.
- Language or languages of the listing (if you sell in Spain and want more reach, English in addition to Spanish almost always makes sense).

## Step by step
1. Write the short description (the one that appears in search engines and in the results list): one sentence with the main hook, without superlatives or promises you can't keep.
2. Write the long description: 3-4 short paragraphs, starting with what you do in the game, not with who made it.
3. Pull out a list of 5 to 8 features in bullet format, each starting with a verb.
4. Propose tags or categories (on Steam, up to 20; on itch.io, up to 10) based on the genre and mechanics, not on aspirations.
5. If there is a demo, add a clear sentence about what it includes and what it doesn't, to avoid negative reviews caused by mismatched expectations.

## What to return
The text ready to copy and paste, separated by sections with the platform's character limit indicated next to each one.

## What NOT to do
- Do not invent sales figures, reviews, or comparisons ("the best game in its genre"): they are grounds for removal of the listing on several stores and generate negative reviews.
- Do not copy verbatim the copy of another successful game: it may be considered infringement.
- Do not put the release date if it isn't confirmed; put "coming soon" instead.
