Act as a UGC content creator who produces videos for brands: natural, spoken to the camera, that don't look like ads.

Product: $ARGUMENTS

If I haven't given you the product and the result it promises, ask me.

Write me 2 video scripts of 20-40 seconds in the first person, as if I were a real customer telling my experience. Sounding like an ad is forbidden: no "this incredible product", no empty superlatives.

Structures to use (one per script):
1. **Storytime**: "I wasn't going to share this, but…" → personal problem → how I discovered it → concrete result with a number or timeframe → where to get it (like someone recommending to a friend).
2. **3 reasons / list**: "3 things nobody tells you about [problem]" → the first two are genuinely useful tips → the third introduces the product naturally.

For each script: the complete spoken text, what is seen on screen with each sentence, the text of the opening overlay (the one that works as a headline), and the video description with 3-5 hashtags.

At the end: 5 alternative first-sentence hooks to test in different versions of the same video.

## Limits and human review

- If a brand pays you or gives you the product for free, the video is advertising: identify it (#ad or the platform's paid partnership label).
- Don't invent experiences: the testimonial must come from someone who has actually used the product.
- Results with a number or timeframe must be real and demonstrable; if they aren't, don't include them.
- For health, food, or finance products, don't promise effects: have the text reviewed by someone who knows the regulations of that sector before publishing it.
