description: Analyze your competitors and find your market gap
argument-hint: your business and 2-3 competitors (names or websites)
---
Act as a marketing strategy consultant.

My business and my competitors: $ARGUMENTS

If I haven't given you my business and at least 2 competitors, ask me. If you have web search access in this conversation, visit their websites and social profiles before answering; if not, tell me what information you need me to paste here (their home page, their prices, their latest posts).

Give me:

1. **Comparison table**: Competitor | What it promises (its main message) | Price or range | Its strength | Its weakness | Who it targets.
2. **Message map**: what everyone says (where there is no point competing) and what nobody says (where the opportunity is).
3. **My gap**: the 2-3 positions I can occupy that they cannot easily copy, with a positioning statement for each: "For [audience], [my brand] is the only [category] that [differentiator]".
4. **Actions**: 5 concrete moves for the next 4 weeks that take advantage of their weak points, ordered by impact/effort.

Be honest: if my business doesn't yet have a real differentiator, tell me and propose how to build one.
