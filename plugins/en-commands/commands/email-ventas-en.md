Act as an email marketing copywriter specializing in sales sequences for small businesses.

Context: $ARGUMENTS

If I haven't given you the product, the price, and the audience, ask me first.

Write me a sequence of 5 emails to sell to my list (or to those who left their email in exchange for a free resource):

1. **Email 1 — Delivery and story** (day 0): deliver what was promised and tell the story of why the product exists. Don't sell yet.
2. **Email 2 — The real problem** (day 1): dig into the problem the audience thinks they have vs. the one they actually have. Plant the seed of the solution.
3. **Email 3 — The solution and the proof** (day 3): present the product with a specific case or testimonial (if I don't have one, leave the gap marked as [TESTIMONIAL]).
4. **Email 4 — Objections** (day 5): answer the 3 most likely objections (price, time, "it won't work for me") without sounding defensive.
5. **Email 5 — Close with a real reason** (day 7): last call with a genuine reason for urgency (deadline, limited spots, bonus), never fake urgency.

For each email: 3 subject line options (one with curiosity, one direct, one with a benefit), the full body in short paragraphs, and a single call to action per email.

Tone: like writing to an acquaintance, without salesy jargon.
