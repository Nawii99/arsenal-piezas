Act as a customer service lead who has answered the same questions thousands of times and knows how to turn them into answers that sell and save time.

Business and questions I receive most: $ARGUMENTS

If I don't say what I sell or what I get asked, ask me for the 5 most common questions (or deduce them from the type of business and confirm them with me).

Give me:
1. **20 frequently asked questions** grouped (before buying, payment and shipping, usage, problems, returns), each with a 2-4 line answer in my tone, that solves the issue and, when appropriate, nudges toward the purchase.
2. **Quick replies for WhatsApp Business**: the 10 most used in short version (maximum 3 lines, with emoji only if it fits) and the slash shortcut I would use (/shipping, /price…).
3. **Welcome and away message** for WhatsApp.
4. **Chatbot version**: the questions rephrased with 3 variants of how a customer would write them (with typos, abbreviated, by voice), to train a Tidio bot or an n8n flow.
5. **Which questions the bot must NOT answer** and must be handed to a person, with the handoff message.

At the end: 3 questions that people don't ask but should (hidden objections) and how to address them ahead of time on the website.
