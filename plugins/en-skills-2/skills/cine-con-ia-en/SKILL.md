# AI Filmmaking

When the user wants video, apply this method without them having to ask.

## 1. A video prompt = 7 ingredients, always in this order
subject and action → setting and time of day → shot type → **camera movement** → lens and depth → lighting and style → duration and aspect ratio.

## 2. Camera movements (the ingredient people forget most)
- `static shot` — still: product, testimonial, on-screen text.
- `slow push-in` — slow approach: creates tension, ideal for the hook.
- `dolly out` — pulling back: reveals context, good closing.
- `orbit / arc shot` — circling around: premium product, cars, sneakers.
- `tracking shot` — following the subject: action, lifestyle.
- `handheld` — handheld camera: believable UGC, testimonial.
- `crane up / drone rise` — rising: scale, landscape, epic ending.
- `whip pan` — quick sweep: energetic transition between shots.

## 3. Shot types
`extreme close-up` (texture), `close-up` (face, product), `medium shot` (bust, use), `wide shot` (context), `over-the-shoulder` (point of view), `top-down` (hands working, food).

## 4. Styles that generators understand
`cinematic, shallow depth of field, 35mm film look` · `commercial product video, studio lighting` · `documentary, natural light, handheld` · `UGC style, shot on iPhone, vertical` · `anamorphic lens flare` (only if the product is tech or "epic").

## 5. Aspect ratios and duration by platform
- Reels / TikTok / Shorts: `9:16`, shots of 2-4 s, total 15-30 s.
- YouTube / web: `16:9`, shots of 3-6 s.
- Meta ads: `4:5` or `9:16`; the hook in the first second.
- Generators produce clips of 5-10 s: plan the video as a list of clips, not as a single take.

## 6. What AI does badly (do not ask for it in the prompt)
Legible text, hands in the foreground, exact logos, more than two people interacting, very fast movements. All of that is added afterward in editing (CapCut or DaVinci Resolve, free).

## 7. Always deliver
Prompts in English ready to paste, an explanation in the user's language, an editing order, and where subtitles, music, and the call to action go. If the user can shoot with a phone, also tell them how to get the same shot without AI.
