# arsenal-en-skills-1

Arsenal del Marketer in English: 20 skills (part 1 of 2) for marketing, sales, design and small business.

## Install

```
/plugin marketplace add Nawii99/arsenal-piezas
/plugin install arsenal-en-skills-1@arsenal
```

## What's inside

| Piece | Area | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| asiento-skill-audio-en | Music & audio | Skill that guides cleaning up a bad voice recording step by step, choosing the right free tool for each problem and in the right order. |
| audio-ffmpeg-sin-tocar-originales-en | Music & audio | Claude writes, tests and runs ffmpeg scripts that convert, level and trim audio batches on copies, leaving originals untouched, ready for podcast hosting upload. |
| cobrar-con-stripe-en | Backend | Claude Code skill that sets up one-off payments and subscriptions with Stripe Checkout, customer portal, webhooks, Spanish VAT and valid invoices, tested in test mode first. |
| entorno-de-pruebas-en | Quality & testing | Claude Code skill with rules for testing tools that send messages, charge money or create contacts without touching real data, with a checklist to pass before going to production. |
| articulos-blog-voz-y-fuentes-en | Content | Writes technical company blog posts in a fixed brand voice, with every fact sourced, and leaves a list of what an expert must verify before publishing. |
| elegir-y-revisar-graficos-en | Data | Chooses the chart or table that best shows a given dataset for the message and audience, and reviews existing charts for axes, colors, titles and mobile readability. |
| hojas-de-calculo-revisables-en | Data | Creates or edits Excel and CSV spreadsheets with working formulas, clear formatting and simple charts, always on a copy, and says what it checked and what you should verify. |
| extraccion-datos-cv-en | Data & AI | Skill that extracts structured candidate data from CV batches using open-source OCR and parsers, delivered as a sorted table. |
| extraer-datos-cv-skill-en | Data & AI | Skill that extracts name, contact, experience, education and languages from CVs in PDF, Word or scanned images into a table. |
| presentaciones-powerpoint-notas-plantilla-en | Design | Creates or edits PowerPoint (.pptx) decks from your content, with clear slides, optional speaker notes and your company template. Opens in PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides. |
| especificacion-edi-odoo-en | Documents | Turns a client's EDIFACT or X12 guides into an Odoo data mapping spec with validation rules, test cases and rollout plan, ready for an integrator to implement. |
| asiento-tienda-legal-en | E-commerce | Skill that checks what an online shop still lacks before opening in Spain: legal texts, VAT-inclusive prices, delivery and returns, cookies and personal data. |
| auditoria-legal-tienda-online-en | E-commerce | Claude Code skill that audits a Spanish online shop's legal pages and produces a prioritized, client-ready report of failures. |
| buscar-skill-para-tarea-de-clase-en | Education | Skill that helps you find an existing skill for a classroom task, such as worksheets, rubrics or presentations, checks that it is safe and licensed, and tests it before use. |
| corregir-lote-ejercicios-en | Education | Skill that grades a folder of student exams or assignments against a rubric, returning a score and feedback for each student plus a group summary. |
| adaptar-curriculum-oferta-en | Jobs & résumé | A skill that adapts a plain-text CV to one specific job offer, reordering sections and highlighting matching keywords and achievements without inventing experience. |
| adaptar-cv-a-oferta-en | Jobs & résumé | A skill that rewrites a CV for one job posting, prioritizing the keywords applicant tracking systems look for, without inventing any experience. |
| cierre-trimestral-autonomo-en | Finance | Skill that prepares the quarterly VAT (form 303) and IRPF (form 130) close for a Spanish freelancer or small company from issued, received and expense invoices. |
| factura-legal-espana-en | Finance | Skill that checks a Spanish invoice for mandatory fields such as numbering, tax IDs, VAT, IRPF withholding and exemption wording, then fixes or drafts it. |
| fotos-producto-ia-en | Photo & image | Skill that applies professional photography language to AI image prompts for product shots, with shot, angle, lens and light. |
