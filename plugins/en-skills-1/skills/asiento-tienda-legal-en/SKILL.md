# Opening a shop in Spain

Use this skill when someone is going to open an online shop and needs to
know what is missing before selling, not after the first complaint.

## Limits (read them before using the output)

- This is a **checklist**, not legal advice. The regulations
  change and the specific case governs.
- Before opening, **an advisory firm (asesoría) or a lawyer has to review** the legal
  texts and the tax treatment. Always say so in the output.
- Do not write clauses as if they were valid: propose drafts and mark them
  as drafts pending review.
- If the business sells to minors, medical devices, food, alcohol,
  supplements, or financial services, **stop and say so**: there are
  specific regulations there that this list does not cover.

## Flow

1. Ask what they sell, to whom (consumer or business), where they invoice from, and
   whether they sell outside Spain.
2. Go through the blocks below and mark each point as done, missing, or not
   applicable. Do not make things up: if you do not know, ask.
3. Order what is missing by risk: first what can cost a penalty or
   a mandatory refund, then the rest.
4. For each missing point, say **who resolves it**: the owner, whoever builds
   the website, the advisory firm, or a lawyer.

## Blocks to review

**Identity and contact.** Legal notice (aviso legal) with the seller's details, an effective means of
contact and, where applicable, commercial registry details.

**Before purchase.** Final price with taxes included and shipping costs
visible before paying; product characteristics; delivery time.

**At purchase.** Order confirmation; the payment button has to make clear
that the purchase carries an obligation to pay.

**After purchase.** Right of withdrawal with its deadline and its form;
warranty; returns policy; what happens if there is no stock.

**Personal data.** What data you collect, for what purpose, how long you keep it,
who you share it with, and how rights are exercised. Record of processing
activities if it applies to you.

**Cookies and measurement.** No analytics or advertising pixels before
consent. Check that the scripts really do not load beforehand.

**Invoicing.** How the invoice is issued, which VAT (IVA) applies depending on where you sell,
and which electronic invoicing obligations apply to you. **The advisory firm
confirms this, without exception.**

## Output

Return: (1) what type of sale it is and to whom, (2) the list of what is missing
ordered by risk, with the person responsible for each point, (3) the text drafts
marked as pending review, (4) the points where a
professional is needed, and (5) the closing sentence: this review does not replace a lawyer
or an advisory firm.
